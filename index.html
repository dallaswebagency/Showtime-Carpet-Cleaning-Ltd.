FAQ

Questions we hear most

Still unsure? Call (403) 608-6265.

How much does professional carpet cleaning cost?

Services start at $250. Final pricing depends on the size of the area and specific treatments needed.

How long does the cleaning process take?

Most standard jobs take 1–3 hours, depending on the number of rooms and level of soiling.

How long does it take for carpets to dry?

Typically 4–6 hours. Humidity and airflow can affect drying time.

Are your cleaning solutions safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use eco-friendly, non-toxic solutions.

What cleaning methods do you use?

Hot Water Extraction, Steam Cleaning, Dry Carpet Cleaning, and Low Moisture Cleaning, chosen per carpet type.

Do you offer commercial services?

Yes, for businesses of all sizes.
